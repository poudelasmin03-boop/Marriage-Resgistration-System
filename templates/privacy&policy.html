Privacy Policy

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how information submitted through the Marriage Registration System is collected, used, and protected.

Information We Collect

  • Name, date of birth, address, phone number, and email address.
  • Marriage-related information provided during registration.
  • Identification and supporting documents submitted by users.
  • Information required to verify and process applications.

How We Use Your Information

  • To process marriage registration applications.
  • To verify the information and documents provided.
  • To communicate with applicants regarding their application.
  • To generate and provide marriage registration certificates.

Information Sharing

Personal information will not be shared unnecessarily with unauthorized individuals or organizations. Information may be accessed by authorized personnel when required for verification and registration purposes.

Your Responsibility

Users are responsible for providing accurate information and protecting their account credentials.

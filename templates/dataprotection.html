Data Protection

We take reasonable measures to protect the personal information and documents submitted through this Marriage Registration System.

Protection of Personal Information

  • Access to personal information is restricted to authorized users.
  • Submitted information is used only for legitimate registration purposes.
  • Users' personal information is not intentionally exposed publicly.
  • Administrative access is restricted to authorized personnel.

Document Security

Documents submitted for marriage registration are handled securely and are accessed only when necessary for application verification and processing.

Account Security

Users should keep their usernames, passwords, and other login credentials confidential and should not share them with others.

Data Retention

Registration information may be retained for as long as necessary to process applications, maintain registration records, and meet applicable administrative requirements.

Data Breach

If a security incident affects personal information, appropriate steps will be taken to investigate and address the issue.

Help & Support

How to Register

Fill in the bride and groom information and upload the required documents.

Required Documents

  • Citizenship / NID of Groom
  • Citizenship / NID of Bride
  • Recent photographs
  • Marriage-related information

Application Status

After submitting your application, the administrator will verify your information and documents.

Certificate

After your application is approved, you receive an email the you can view your certificated.

Application Rejected?

If your application is rejected, review the provided information and documents and submit the application again.

Need More Help?

Contact the Marriage Registration Office for further assistance.

About the System

This E-Governance Marriage Registration System allows users to submit marriage registration applications online.

Registration Process

  1. Fill in the marriage registration form.
  2. Enter bride and groom information.
  3. Upload required documents.
  4. Submit the application.
  5. Administrator verifies the application.
  6. Application is approved or rejected.
  7. Certificate is generated after approval.

Required Information

  • Bride's personal information
  • Groom's personal information
  • Citizenship / NID information
  • Marriage date
  • Marriage location
  • Contact information

Certificate

Once the administrator approves the application, a unique marriage certificate number is generated and the certificate can be viewed online.
